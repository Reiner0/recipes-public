## Ingredients

- ¼ cup (60ml) low-sodium soy sauce
- ½ cup (120ml) water, plus 2 tablespoons (30ml) for slurry
- 2 tablespoons (30ml) mirin
- 2 tablespoons (25g) brown sugar
- 1 garlic clove (3g), finely grated
- 1 teaspoon (2g) finely grated fresh ginger
- 1 tablespoon (8g) cornstarch

**Sodium notes:** Soy sauce supplies sodium; use low-sodium soy diluted with water, not regular soy or salted broth. Add no salt.

## Instructions

1. **Simmer base:** Combine soy, ½ cup (120ml) water, mirin, sugar, garlic and ginger in a small saucepan. Simmer over medium-low heat for 3 minutes, stirring, until sugar dissolves and garlic smells mellow.

2. **Thicken sauce:** Mix cornstarch with remaining 2 tablespoons (30ml) cold water. Stir into the simmering sauce and cook 1–2 minutes, until glossy and lightly coats a spoon. Add a splash of water if it gets too thick.

3. **Cool and store:** Makes about 1 cup (240ml), or six sauce portions of about 2½ tablespoons (40ml). Cool promptly, cover and refrigerate within 2 hours; use within 4 days or freeze half immediately for the next rotation.

## Notes

Use ½ cup (120ml) for one family batch of [Chicken Teriyaki Rice Bowls](../mains/chicken-teriyaki-bowls.md); freeze the other half for up to 3 months. Mild base; add heat only at the table.
