## Ingredients

- ¼ cup (28g) sweet paprika, not hot or smoked-hot paprika
- 2 tablespoons (12g) ground cumin
- 2 tablespoons (18g) garlic powder, not garlic salt
- 2 tablespoons (16g) onion powder, not onion salt
- 1 tablespoon (3g) dried oregano
- 1 tablespoon (8g) cornstarch
- No added salt, cayenne or chili powder blend

**Sodium notes:** Commercial taco packets often supply salt; replace them with this salt-free jar and use garlic/onion powder rather than seasoned salts.

## Instructions

1. **Mix jar:** Whisk all ingredients until the color is even and no cornstarch clumps remain. Transfer to a clean, dry airtight jar and label with the date.

2. **Measure per pound:** Use 2 tablespoons (about 14g) per 1 lb (450g) ground meat, plus ⅓ cup (80ml) water. Stir into fully browned meat and simmer 3–5 minutes, until coated and no powder remains. Turkey must reach 165°F (74°C).

3. **Store dry:** Makes about ¾ cup (85g), enough to season six 1 lb (450g) batches. Keep dry and away from heat; use within 3 months for best flavor.

## Notes

For [Turkey Taco Rice Bowls](../mains/turkey-taco-rice-bowls.md), use 3 tablespoons (about 21g) for 1½ lb (680g) turkey. This has no spicy ingredient; adults can add hot sauce to their own bowls.
