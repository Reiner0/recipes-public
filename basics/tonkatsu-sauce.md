## Ingredients

- ¼ cup (60g) no-salt-added ketchup
- 1 tablespoon (15ml) Worcestershire sauce
- 1 teaspoon (5ml) low-sodium soy sauce
- 1 teaspoon (4g) sugar
- 1 teaspoon (5ml) water, if needed

**Sodium notes:** Worcestershire and soy are the main sources; choose lower-sodium Worcestershire where available, low-sodium soy and no-salt-added ketchup. Add no salt.

## Instructions

1. **Mix sauce:** Stir ketchup, Worcestershire, soy and sugar in a small bowl until smooth with no sugar grit. Add the measured water if needed for a spoonable consistency.

2. **Serve separately:** Makes about ⅓ cup (80ml), four portions of about 1 tablespoon plus 1 teaspoon (20ml). Serve alongside [Chicken Katsu](../mains/chicken-katsu.md) so the kids can dip.

3. **Chill leftovers:** Cover and refrigerate; use within 5 days. Use a clean spoon each time.

## Notes

Mild and lightly sweet. Adult heat belongs at the table, not in this sauce.
