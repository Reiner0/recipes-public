## Ingredients

- 1 can (12 oz / 340g) Spam 25% Less Sodium, cut into thin strips
- 12 large eggs
- 1½ cups (285g) dry rice and 1½ cups (360ml) water, or 4½ cups (720g) cooked rice
- 1 teaspoon (5ml) neutral oil
- Optional 1 teaspoon (3g) furikake for the whole dish
- For the adults: optional 1 teaspoon (5ml) sriracha per bowl

**Sodium notes:** Spam is still salty even with 25% less sodium; use one can for four, add no salt or soy sauce, and omit furikake for the lowest-sodium version. Eggs supply much of the protein.

## Instructions

1. **Start rice:** Rinse the rice. Put it and the measured water in the Instant Pot; cook at HIGH pressure for 3 minutes, then natural-release for 10 minutes. Release any remaining pressure and fluff; the grains should be tender. Or reheat the same amount of cooked batch rice until steaming throughout.

2. **Brown Spam:** Put strips in a dry nonstick or well-seasoned skillet over medium heat. Cook 5–7 minutes, turning, until browned at the edges. Transfer to a plate.

3. **Cook eggs:** Add oil to the skillet. Scramble eggs in two batches for 3–4 minutes each, until fully set with no wet streaks, or fry until whites and yolks are firm.

4. **Assemble bowls:** Divide rice and Spam among four bowls. Give each adult four eggs and each child two. Add optional furikake sparingly; keep adult sriracha separate.

## Notes

**From frozen:** Pantry backup: nothing needs thawing. Use shelf-stable unopened Spam and refrigerated eggs; frozen cooked rice can reheat covered with 2 tablespoons (30ml) water until 165°F (74°C) throughout.

**Weeknight timing:** About 20–25 minutes active. Four eggs plus a quarter-can of Spam and rice gives each adult roughly 40g protein. This is the occasional salty backup, rather than the lowest-sodium dinner.
