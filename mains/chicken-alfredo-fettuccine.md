## Ingredients

- 8 oz fettuccine (half a 16 oz box)
- 1½ lb diced chicken breast
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 2–3 teaspoons minced garlic
- 1 jar (15 oz) garlic alfredo sauce
- Salt, for the pasta water

## Instructions

1. **Boil pasta:** Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the fettuccine per package instructions. Before draining, reserve about 1 cup of the pasta water.

2. **Brown chicken:** Meanwhile, heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the diced chicken and cook until browned and cooked through.

3. **Add garlic:** Stir in the minced garlic and cook about 1 minute, until fragrant.

4. **Add sauce:** Pour in the alfredo sauce, stir to coat, and reduce heat to low to keep warm while the pasta finishes.

5. **Combine:** Add the drained fettuccine to the skillet and toss to coat. Add reserved pasta water a splash at a time until everything is glossy and well combined.

## Notes

- Uses half a 16 oz box of fettuccine; the other half keeps for next time.
- Pre-diced chicken breast keeps this a true weeknight meal.
- Reserved pasta water is the trick for loosening jarred sauce into a silky coat.
