## Ingredients

- 1½ lb (680g) lean ground turkey, thawed
- 1 tablespoon (15ml) olive oil
- 3 tablespoons (about 21g) [Low-Sodium Taco Seasoning](../basics/low-sodium-taco-seasoning.md)
- ½ cup (120ml) water
- 1½ cups (285g) dry rice and 1½ cups (360ml) water, or 4½ cups (720g) cooked rice
- 1 can (15 oz / 425g) no-salt-added black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup (150g) frozen corn
- ½ cup (55g) shredded cheddar
- ½ cup (120g) plain Greek yogurt or sour cream
- For the adults: optional 1 teaspoon (5ml) hot sauce per bowl

**Sodium notes:** Cheese is the main source; measure it, use no-salt-added beans and the salt-free seasoning. Greek yogurt adds protein without extra salty toppings.

## Instructions

1. **Start rice:** Rinse the rice. Put it and the measured water in the Instant Pot; cook at HIGH pressure for 3 minutes, then natural-release for 10 minutes. Release any remaining pressure and fluff; the grains should be tender. Or reheat the same amount of cooked batch rice until steaming throughout.

2. **Brown turkey:** Heat oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add turkey and break it into small crumbles. Cook 7–9 minutes, until no pink remains and the meat reaches 165°F (74°C).

3. **Season gently:** Stir in seasoning and ½ cup (120ml) water. Add beans and corn; simmer 5 minutes, until hot throughout and the liquid lightly coats the meat.

4. **Build bowls:** Set out rice, turkey mixture, measured cheese and yogurt separately. Let the kids assemble their bowls. Add hot sauce only to adult bowls.

## Notes

**From frozen:** Freeze turkey in two flat ¾ lb (340g) bags. Submerge leakproof bags in cold tap water; change the water every 30 minutes. Allow 30–60 minutes per flat 1 lb (450g) bag, longer if the center is still icy. Cook immediately after cold-water thawing. Never thaw on the counter.

**Weeknight timing:** About 25 minutes active. Roughly 40g protein per equal serving with Greek yogurt; add another spoonful for a simple boost.
