## Ingredients

- 1½ lb (680g) lean ground turkey, thawed
- 2 garlic cloves (6g), minced
- 1 large egg
- ½ cup (30g) panko breadcrumbs
- ½ teaspoon (3g) fine salt
- 1 tablespoon (15ml) neutral oil
- 1½ cups (285g) dry rice and 1½ cups (360ml) water, or 4½ cups (720g) cooked rice
- Optional mild glaze: 1 tablespoon (15ml) low-sodium soy sauce, 1 tablespoon (21g) honey and 2 tablespoons (30ml) water
- For the adults: spicy mayo (store-bought, already on hand), optional 1 tablespoon (15ml) per bowl

**Sodium notes:** Panko, soy glaze and mayo supply sodium; choose lower-sodium panko, use low-sodium soy, and serve glaze/mayo separately. Salt is limited to ½ teaspoon (3g) for the batch (about 290mg per serving).

## Instructions

1. **Heat oven and rice:** Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Rinse rice and cook with its measured water in the Instant Pot at HIGH pressure for 3 minutes; natural-release for 10 minutes, release the rest, and fluff.

2. **Shape meatballs:** Gently mix turkey, garlic, egg, panko and measured salt. Form 20 meatballs, about 1½ inches (4cm) wide. Brush a sheet pan with the oil and arrange them with space between.

3. **Bake meatballs:** Bake for 15–18 minutes, until lightly browned, firm and 165°F (74°C) in the center. Alternatively, use the oil in a skillet: brown in two batches for 8–10 minutes each, turning often; cover with 2 tablespoons (30ml) water per batch until cooked through.

4. **Glaze and serve:** If using glaze, simmer its three ingredients in a skillet for 2–3 minutes, until lightly syrupy. Toss in cooked meatballs or serve the glaze separately. Serve over rice; put spicy mayo on adult bowls only.

## Notes

**From frozen:** For raw turkey, freeze in two ¾ lb (340g) flat bags. Submerge leakproof bags in cold tap water; change the water every 30 minutes. Allow 30–60 minutes per flat 1 lb (450g) bag, longer if the center is still icy. Cook immediately after cold-water thawing. Never thaw on the counter.

**Make ahead:** Cook meatballs fully, cool promptly and refrigerate within 2 hours. Refrigerate up to 3–4 days, or freeze individually on a sheet pan, then bag for up to 3 months. Reheat from frozen in a covered skillet with ¼ cup (60ml) water for 12–18 minutes, turning occasionally, until the centers reach 165°F (74°C). Uncover to evaporate extra water.

**Weeknight timing:** About 35 minutes active from raw turkey, or 20 minutes with cooked meatballs. Roughly 36g protein per equal serving.
