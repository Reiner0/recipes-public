## Ingredients

- 1 lb spaghetti
- 4 slices bacon, diced
- 4 large eggs
- 2 oz heavy cream
- 4 oz grated Parmesan, plus more for serving
- 4 teaspoons minced garlic
- Fresh parsley, chopped, for garnish
- Salt, for the pasta water

## Instructions

1. **Boil pasta:** Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the spaghetti per package instructions. Before draining, reserve about 1 cup of the pasta water.

2. **Make the egg mixture:** In a bowl, whisk together the eggs, heavy cream, and grated Parmesan. Set aside.

3. **Fry bacon:** Meanwhile, dice and fry the bacon in a large skillet until crisp. Drain, leaving a little of the hot grease in the skillet.

4. **Cook garlic:** Add the minced garlic to the skillet and cook about 1 minute, until fragrant, then return the bacon to the pan.

5. **Combine off heat:** Add the drained spaghetti and toss. Turn off the heat, then pour in the egg/cream/Parmesan mixture, tossing constantly so it forms a creamy sauce without scrambling. Add reserved pasta water a splash at a time until silky.

6. **Serve:** Top with chopped parsley and extra grated Parmesan.

## Notes

- A bacon-driven carbonara. The eggs must go in **off the heat** and be tossed constantly — residual warmth cooks them into a sauce; direct heat scrambles them.
- Reserved pasta water is essential for loosening the sauce to the right consistency.
- Keeping a little bacon grease in the pan carries the flavor into the garlic and sauce.
