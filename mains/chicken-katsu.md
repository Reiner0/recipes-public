## Ingredients

- 1½ lb (680g) boneless chicken breast, divided into 4–6 cutlets pounded to ¼ inch (6mm), thawed
- ½ cup (60g) all-purpose flour
- 2 large eggs, beaten
- 1½ cups (90g) panko breadcrumbs
- ¼ teaspoon (1.5g) fine salt
- ½ cup (120ml) neutral oil for shallow frying (about ¼ inch / 6mm deep in a 12-inch skillet)
- 1½ cups (285g) dry rice and 1½ cups (360ml) water, or 4½ cups (720g) cooked rice
- ½ small cabbage (8 oz / 225g), finely shredded
- 1 batch (about ⅓ cup / 80ml) [Tonkatsu Sauce](../basics/tonkatsu-sauce.md)
- For the adults: optional 1 teaspoon (5ml) sriracha per plate

**Sodium notes:** Panko and sauce supply sodium; choose lower-sodium panko and no-salt-added ketchup in the linked sauce. Keep sauce on the side and limit added salt to ¼ teaspoon (1.5g).

## Instructions

1. **Start rice:** Rinse the rice. Put it and the measured water in the Instant Pot; cook at HIGH pressure for 3 minutes, then natural-release for 10 minutes. Release any remaining pressure and fluff; the grains should be tender. Or reheat the same amount of cooked batch rice until steaming throughout.

2. **Bread cutlets:** Pat cutlets dry and sprinkle the measured salt over all of them. Put flour, beaten eggs and panko in three shallow dishes. Coat each cutlet in flour, then egg, then panko, pressing gently; discard unused breading that touched raw chicken.

3. **Shallow-fry chicken:** Heat oil in a wide skillet over medium heat until a breadcrumb sizzles gently. Fry cutlets in two batches for 2–3 minutes per side, until crisp and golden and the thickest part reaches 165°F (74°C). Lower the heat if the crust browns before the center cooks.

4. **Serve crisp:** Rest on a paper-towel-lined plate for 2 minutes, then slice. Serve with rice, shredded cabbage and sauce on the side. Leave sriracha for adults.

## Notes

**From frozen:** Pound cutlets before freezing. Bag them flat in single layers, in two ¾ lb (340g) dinner bags. A thin bag may thaw in about 30 minutes in cold water; allow up to 60 minutes or longer if any ice remains. Keep bags sealed, change cold water every 30 minutes and cook immediately. Never counter-thaw.

**Weeknight timing:** About 35 minutes active; thawing is extra unattended time. Roughly 40g protein per equal serving.
