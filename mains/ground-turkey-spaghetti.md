## Ingredients

- 1½ lb (680g) lean ground turkey, thawed
- 12 oz (340g) dry spaghetti
- 1 medium onion (150g), finely diced
- 3 garlic cloves (9g), minced
- 1 tablespoon (15ml) olive oil
- 1 can (28 oz / 795g) no-salt-added crushed tomatoes
- 2 teaspoons (2g) dried Italian herbs, without salt
- ½ teaspoon (3g) fine salt for sauce; no salt for pasta water
- ¼ cup (20g) grated parmesan
- For the adults: optional ¼ teaspoon (0.5g) red pepper flakes per plate

**Sodium notes:** Parmesan is the main source; measure ¼ cup (20g) for the whole dish and use no-salt-added tomatoes, salt-free herbs and unsalted pasta water.

## Instructions

1. **Boil pasta:** Bring a pot of unsalted water to a boil. Cook spaghetti according to the package, until tender with a slight bite. Reserve ½ cup (120ml) cooking water, then drain.

2. **Brown turkey:** Meanwhile, heat oil in a wide skillet. Add onion and cook 3 minutes, until softened. Add turkey and break into crumbles; cook 7–9 minutes, until no pink remains and it reaches 165°F (74°C).

3. **Simmer sauce:** Add garlic and cook 1 minute, until fragrant. Stir in tomatoes, herbs and measured salt. Simmer 8–10 minutes, until slightly thickened.

4. **Toss and finish:** Toss pasta with sauce; add reserved water a little at a time if needed. Divide parmesan over four plates. Adults can add pepper flakes separately.

## Notes

**From frozen:** Freeze turkey in two flat ¾ lb (340g) bags. Submerge leakproof bags in cold tap water; change the water every 30 minutes. Allow 30–60 minutes per flat 1 lb (450g) bag, longer if the center is still icy. Cook immediately after cold-water thawing. Never thaw on the counter.

**Weeknight timing:** About 30 minutes active. Roughly 40g protein per equal serving; a spoonful of plain Greek yogurt can soften the sauce and add protein.
