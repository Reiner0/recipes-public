## Ingredients

- 2 lb (900g) lean ground turkey, thawed
- 1 box (14.5 oz / 410g) protein spaghetti (such as Barilla Protein+) or regular spaghetti
- 1 medium onion (150g), finely diced
- 3 garlic cloves (9g), minced
- 1 tablespoon (15ml) olive oil
- 1 jar (24 oz / 680g) Rao's marinara sauce
- 1 teaspoon (6g) fine salt for the pasta water
- ¼ cup (20g) grated parmesan
- For the adults: optional ¼ teaspoon (0.5g) red pepper flakes per plate

**Sodium notes:** The marinara and parmesan carry the salt, so the sauce gets no added salt. For a lower-sodium night, swap in no-salt-added crushed tomatoes plus 2 teaspoons dried Italian herbs and ½ teaspoon salt.

## Instructions

1. **Boil pasta:** Bring a pot of water to a boil and add the measured salt. Cook spaghetti according to the package, until tender with a slight bite. Reserve ½ cup (120ml) cooking water, then drain.

2. **Brown turkey:** Meanwhile, heat oil in a wide skillet. Add onion and cook 3 minutes, until softened. Add turkey and break into crumbles; cook 7–9 minutes, until no pink remains and it reaches 165°F (74°C).

3. **Simmer sauce:** Add garlic and cook 1 minute, until fragrant. Stir in the marinara. Simmer 5–8 minutes, until hot and the turkey is coated.

4. **Toss and finish:** Toss pasta with sauce; add reserved water a little at a time if needed. Divide parmesan over four plates. Adults can add pepper flakes separately.

## Notes

**From frozen:** Freeze turkey in two flat 1 lb (450g) bags. Submerge leakproof bags in cold tap water; change the water every 30 minutes. Allow 30–60 minutes per flat 1 lb (450g) bag, longer if the center is still icy. Cook immediately after cold-water thawing. Never thaw on the counter.

**Weeknight timing:** About 30 minutes active. Roughly 40g protein per equal serving; a spoonful of plain Greek yogurt can soften the sauce and add protein.
