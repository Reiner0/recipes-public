## Ingredients

- 2 slices bacon
- 4 large eggs
- 2 heads butter lettuce, chopped
- 1 can (12.5 oz) chicken breast, drained
- 1 cup grape tomatoes, halved
- ½ cup crumbled blue cheese
- ½ cup shredded cheddar cheese
- Ranch dressing, to serve

## Instructions

1. **Cook bacon:** Fry the bacon in a skillet over medium heat until crisp, 8 to 10 minutes. Drain on paper towels, then chop or crumble.

2. **Hard-boil eggs:** Place eggs on a trivet in the Instant Pot with 1 cup water. Cook at HIGH pressure for 8 minutes, quick-release, then transfer to an ice-water bath for a few minutes. Peel and quarter (or chop).

3. **Assemble:** Spread the chopped butter lettuce in a bowl or platter. Arrange the chicken, tomatoes, bacon, eggs, blue cheese, and shredded cheddar in rows or piles on top.

4. **Serve:** Dress with ranch (or serve dressing on the side).

## Notes

- The 8-min/quick-release/ice-bath Instant Pot method gives easy-peel eggs with fully set yolks.
- Canned chicken keeps this fast; swap in leftover rotisserie or grilled chicken if you have it.
- Everything but the bacon and eggs can be prepped ahead; assemble just before serving.
