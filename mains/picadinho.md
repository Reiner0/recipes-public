## Ingredients

- 1½ lb (680g) lean ground beef, thawed; or tender sirloin cut into ½-inch (1cm) cubes
- 1 medium onion (150g), diced
- 3 garlic cloves (9g), minced
- 1 lb (450g) potatoes, cut into ½-inch (1cm) cubes
- 2 medium carrots (8 oz / 225g), cut into small cubes
- 1 can (14½ oz / 410g) no-salt-added diced tomatoes with juices
- ½ cup (120ml) water, plus 2 tablespoons (30ml) if needed
- 1 tablespoon (15ml) olive oil
- ½ teaspoon (3g) fine salt
- 4 servings [Brazilian Rice and Beans](../sides/brazilian-rice-and-beans.md), prepared ahead
- For the adults: optional 1 teaspoon (5ml) hot sauce per plate

**Sodium notes:** Added salt is the main source; measure ½ teaspoon (3g), use no-salt-added tomatoes and water instead of salted broth.

## Instructions

1. **Brown beef:** Heat oil in a wide skillet over medium-high heat. Add beef and brown for 5–7 minutes, breaking ground beef into crumbles. For diced sirloin, brown briefly and transfer to a plate; avoid stew beef, which needs a long braise.

2. **Soften aromatics:** Add onion and garlic to the skillet. Cook 3 minutes, until the onion softens and garlic smells fragrant.

3. **Simmer vegetables:** Stir in potatoes, carrots, tomatoes, water and salt. Cover and simmer for 15–18 minutes, stirring occasionally, until a fork easily pierces the vegetables. Add the extra water if the pan dries out. Ground beef must reach 160°F (71°C).

4. **Finish and serve:** If using sirloin, return it for the last 3–5 minutes; check several pieces reach 145°F (63°C), then rest off the heat for 3 minutes. Reheat prepared rice and beans until steaming throughout and 165°F (74°C). Serve alongside the skillet; keep hot sauce separate.

## Notes

**From frozen:** Freeze ground beef or diced sirloin in two flat ¾ lb (340g) bags. Submerge leakproof bags in cold tap water; change the water every 30 minutes. Allow 30–60 minutes per flat 1 lb (450g) bag, longer if the center is still icy. Cook immediately after cold-water thawing. Never thaw on the counter.

**Weeknight timing:** About 35 minutes active with rice and beans already cooked. Ground beef plus beans provides roughly 40g protein per equal serving.
