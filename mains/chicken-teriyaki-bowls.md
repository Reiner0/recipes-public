## Ingredients

- 1½ lb (680g) raw-weight chicken from [Sous Vide Batch Chicken](../basics/sous-vide-batch-chicken.md), or thawed boneless, skinless breasts or thighs
- 1½ cups (285g) dry jasmine rice and 1½ cups (360ml) water, or 4½ cups (720g) cooked rice
- 1 lb (450g) broccoli florets
- 1 tablespoon (15ml) neutral oil
- ½ cup (120ml) [Low-Sodium Teriyaki Sauce](../basics/low-sodium-teriyaki-sauce.md)
- For the adults: optional 1 teaspoon (5ml) sriracha per bowl

**Sodium notes:** Teriyaki supplies the sodium; use the linked low-sodium sauce and no additional salt.

## Instructions

1. **Start rice:** Rinse the rice. Put it and the measured water in the Instant Pot; cook at HIGH pressure for 3 minutes, then natural-release for 10 minutes. Release any remaining pressure and fluff; the grains should be tender. Or reheat the same amount of cooked batch rice until steaming throughout.

2. **Steam broccoli:** Put broccoli in a covered saucepan with ¼ cup (60ml) water. Steam for 5–7 minutes, until bright green and fork-tender; drain.

3. **Brown chicken:** Pat chicken dry. Heat oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. For batch chicken, sear 1–2 minutes per side until golden, then slice. For raw breasts, slice horizontally into thin cutlets and sear 4–5 minutes per side; raw thighs take 5–7 minutes per side. Either is done when browned and 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part.

4. **Glaze and serve:** Add sauce and sliced chicken to the skillet; simmer until glossy. For refrigerated batch chicken, cover and heat until the center reaches 165°F (74°C), adding 2 tablespoons (30ml) water if needed. Serve with rice and broccoli, with sauce on the side for picky eaters.

## Notes

**Not a teriyaki fan?** Homemade sauce is thinner and less sweet than bottled. Glaze only the kids' portion and serve the rest plain with sauce on the side; spicy mayo works too.

**From frozen:** Cook straight from frozen using the [batch method](../basics/sous-vide-batch-chicken.md) on Sunday; its times already include the frozen allowance. For the pan route, freeze in two ¾ lb (340g) flat bags. Submerge leakproof bags in cold tap water; change the water every 30 minutes. Allow 30–60 minutes per flat 1 lb (450g) bag, longer if the center is still icy. Cook immediately after cold-water thawing. Never thaw on the counter.

**Weeknight timing:** About 25–30 minutes active; batch cooking or thawing is separate. Roughly 33–38g protein per equal serving.
