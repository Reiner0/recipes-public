## Ingredients

- 1½ lb (680g) boneless, skinless chicken thighs, thawed and cut into ¾-inch (2cm) pieces
- 1½ cups (285g) dry jasmine rice and 1½ cups (360ml) water, or 4½ cups (720g) cooked rice
- 1 medium onion (150g), thinly sliced
- 6 large eggs, lightly beaten
- 1 cup (240ml) unsalted dashi (kombu/bonito broth, not salted instant powder), or low-sodium chicken broth
- 2 tablespoons (30ml) low-sodium soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons (30ml) mirin
- 2 teaspoons (8g) sugar
- For the adults: optional 1 teaspoon (5ml) chili crisp per bowl

**Sodium notes:** Soy sauce is the main source; use low-sodium soy and unsalted dashi. Add no extra salt.

## Instructions

1. **Start rice:** Rinse the rice. Put it and the measured water in the Instant Pot; cook at HIGH pressure for 3 minutes, then natural-release for 10 minutes. Release any remaining pressure and fluff; the grains should be tender. Or reheat the same amount of cooked batch rice until steaming throughout.

2. **Simmer broth:** Combine dashi, soy sauce, mirin, sugar and onion in a wide skillet. Simmer over medium heat for 5 minutes, until the onion softens. Dashi is the broth; soy and mirin season it.

3. **Cook chicken:** Add the chicken in one layer. Cover and simmer gently for 8–10 minutes, stirring once, until the thickest pieces reach 165°F (74°C) and no raw-looking center remains.

4. **Set eggs:** Pour the eggs evenly around the chicken. Cover and cook on low for 3–5 minutes, until the eggs are fully set and reach 160°F (71°C); leave no runny egg for the kids.

5. **Serve bowls:** Divide rice among four bowls and spoon chicken, egg and broth over it. Keep adult chili crisp at the table.

## Notes

**From frozen:** Freeze thighs flat in two ¾ lb (340g) bags. Submerge leakproof bags in cold tap water; change the water every 30 minutes. Allow 30–60 minutes per flat 1 lb (450g) bag, longer if the center is still icy. Cook immediately after cold-water thawing. Never thaw on the counter.

**Weeknight timing:** About 30 minutes active; thawing is extra unattended time. Roughly 40g protein per equal serving; an extra egg is an easy boost.
