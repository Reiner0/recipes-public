## Ingredients

- 4½ cups (720g) cooked day-old rice, refrigerated promptly
- 8 large eggs, beaten
- 12 oz (340g) cooked leftover chicken, salmon or turkey meatballs, chopped; or Spam 25% Less Sodium, diced
- 2 cups (280g) frozen peas and carrots
- 3 scallions (45g), sliced
- 2 tablespoons (30ml) neutral oil, divided
- 1 tablespoon (15ml) low-sodium soy sauce
- For the adults: optional 1 teaspoon (5ml) chili crisp per bowl

**Sodium notes:** Soy and leftover seasoning (especially Spam) are the sources; use low-sodium soy, unsalted batch chicken and no added salt. Omit soy when using Spam.

## Instructions

1. **Loosen rice:** Break cold rice into small clumps. Chop the cooked protein and scallions before heating the pan.

2. **Scramble eggs:** Heat 1 tablespoon (15ml) oil in a wide skillet over medium heat. Scramble eggs for 2–3 minutes, until fully set with no wet streaks. Transfer to a plate.

3. **Fry rice:** Add remaining oil, vegetables and scallions. Cook 3 minutes, until vegetables soften. Add rice and protein; stir-fry 5–7 minutes until steaming, breaking up clumps. Work in two batches if the pan is crowded.

4. **Finish gently:** Fold in eggs and soy (omit soy for Spam). Heat until the rice and leftover protein reach 165°F (74°C) throughout. Serve adult chili crisp separately.

## Notes

**From frozen:** Use cooked leftovers, never raw frozen meat. Reheat frozen chopped cooked protein in a covered skillet with 2 tablespoons (30ml) water for 8–12 minutes to 165°F (74°C) before adding; allow longer for large meatballs. Frozen vegetables go straight into the pan.

**Rice safety:** Cool rice in shallow containers and refrigerate within 2 hours at 40°F (4°C) or below; use within 3–4 days or freeze promptly. Reheating cannot rescue rice left warm too long.

**Weeknight timing:** About 25 minutes active. Chicken or turkey gives roughly 35–40g protein per equal serving; with Spam, add an extra fully cooked egg to each adult bowl to reach about 30g.
