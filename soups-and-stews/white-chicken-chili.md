## Ingredients

**Chili**
- 1 tablespoon (15ml) olive oil
- ½ small yellow onion (75g), diced
- 3 cloves garlic (9g), finely minced, or 1 tablespoon (15g) jar minced garlic
- 4 cups (950ml) low-sodium chicken broth
- 2 teaspoons (5g) ground cumin
- ½ teaspoon (1g) paprika
- ½ teaspoon (0.5g) dried oregano
- ½ teaspoon (3g) salt
- ½ teaspoon (1g) black pepper
- 1 cup (150g) frozen corn
- 2 cans (15 oz / 425g each) no-salt-added cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
- 2–3 cups (280–420g) shredded cooked chicken (one 1½ lb / 680g [batch chicken](../basics/sous-vide-batch-chicken.md) portion)

**Finish**
- 1 cup (240ml) heavy cream
- 2 tablespoons (16g) cornstarch
- 1 lime, juiced (about 2 tablespoons / 30ml)
- ¼ cup (10g) chopped cilantro, divided

**Toppings and optional heat**
- 1 cup (115g) shredded Monterey Jack, measured for the table
- Optional: 1 can (7 oz / 200g) diced green chiles

**Sodium notes:** The source recipe uses regular broth, regular beans, 1 teaspoon salt and 2 cups cheese. This version uses low-sodium broth, no-salt-added beans, half the salt and half the cheese; taste before adding more at the end.

## Instructions

1. **Soften aromatics:** Heat the oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add the onion and sauté 4–6 minutes, until soft and translucent. Stir in the garlic and cook about 30 seconds, until fragrant.

2. **Build the chili:** Add the broth, cumin, paprika, oregano, salt, pepper, corn, beans and chicken. If the whole family is having chiles, stir them in now; otherwise leave them out (step 6).

3. **Simmer:** Bring to a boil, then cover, reduce to medium-low and simmer 15–20 minutes, until the corn is tender and the flavors meld. If using batch chicken from the fridge, make sure it reaches 165°F (74°C).

4. **Thicken:** Remove the lid. Whisk the cornstarch into the cream in a small bowl until smooth, then stir it into the pot with the lime juice and 2 tablespoons of the cilantro.

5. **Finish:** Simmer a few minutes, stirring, until the chili coats a spoon and the cream is hot. Too thick? Add a splash of broth or water. Too thin? Mash some beans against the side of the pot.

6. **Serve:** Top with the remaining cilantro and the measured cheese. For a chile-free pot, warm the green chiles in a small bowl in the microwave and spoon them over the adult bowls.

## Notes

**From frozen:** Use a 1½ lb (680g) portion of [Sous Vide Batch Chicken](../basics/sous-vide-batch-chicken.md); move it from the freezer to the fridge the night before, then shred it. No cooked chicken? Simmer 1½ lb (680g) raw breasts in the broth for 15–20 minutes, to 165°F (74°C), then shred and return them.

**Variations:** For more heat, use hot green chiles, a chopped chipotle or ½ teaspoon cayenne in adult bowls. For extra creaminess, stir in 2 oz (55g) diced cream cheese with the cream. Pepper Jack or sharp cheddar can replace the Monterey Jack.

**Leftovers:** Refrigerate within 2 hours and use within 3–4 days. It thickens as it cools; loosen with broth when reheating. Cream-thickened soup can turn grainy after freezing, so eat leftovers within the week.
