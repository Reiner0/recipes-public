## Ingredients

- 1 lb (450g) dried pinto or black beans, sorted and rinsed
- 6 cups (1.4L) water for beans
- 1 medium onion (150g), diced
- 7 garlic cloves (21g), minced, divided: 4 for beans and 3 for rice
- 2 bay leaves
- 3 tablespoons (45ml) olive oil, divided: 2 for beans and 1 for rice
- ½ teaspoon (3g) fine salt for beans, added after cooking
- 2 cups (380g) dry long-grain white rice
- 3 cups (720ml) water for rice
- ¼ teaspoon (1.5g) fine salt for rice

**Sodium notes:** Measured finishing salt is the source; start the beans without salt and use water instead of salted broth. No bacon or salty seasoning mix.

## Instructions

1. **Start beans:** Use a 6-quart (5.7L) or larger Instant Pot. On SAUTE, heat 2 tablespoons (30ml) oil; soften onion for 3 minutes, then add 4 garlic cloves for 30 seconds. Add beans, 6 cups (1.4L) water and bay leaves. Stay below the half-full mark for expanding beans; halve the batch if necessary.

2. **Pressure-cook beans:** Cook at HIGH pressure for 30 minutes for black beans or 35 minutes for pintos. Allow 20 minutes natural release, then carefully vent. Beans should crush easily with a fork and have creamy centers; if firm, pressure-cook another 5–10 minutes with enough liquid to cover, then natural-release 10 minutes.

3. **Season beans:** Remove bay leaves. Stir in ½ teaspoon (3g) salt. Mash ½ cup (90g) beans against the side of the pot and simmer on SAUTE for 3–5 minutes, stirring, until the broth lightly thickens.

4. **Make garlic rice:** Meanwhile, heat remaining oil in a saucepan over medium-low heat. Sauté 3 garlic cloves briefly, about 30 seconds, until fragrant but not browned. Add rinsed, drained rice, 3 cups (720ml) water and ¼ teaspoon (1.5g) salt; bring to a boil. Cover, turn to low and simmer 18 minutes, until the water is absorbed. Fluff, then cover and rest 5 minutes off the heat.

5. **Portion for dinners:** Serve about 1 cup (160g) cooked rice and ¾–1 cup (135–180g) beans per portion. Expect about 6 cups (960g) rice and 6 cups (1.1kg) beans; freeze the extra two portions after serving four.

## Notes

**From frozen:** No meat to thaw. Reheat frozen cooked beans in a covered saucepan with ¼ cup (60ml) water per 2 cups (360g) beans for 10–15 minutes, stirring, until 165°F (74°C); reheat frozen rice covered until equally hot.

**Make ahead / five-day plan:** Cool in shallow containers and refrigerate within 2 hours at 40°F (4°C) or below. Cooked rice and beans keep 3–4 days in the fridge, not five; freeze the day-five portions on prep day, thaw overnight in the fridge and reheat once to 165°F (74°C). Freeze up to 3 months.

**Timing:** About 20 minutes active, 75–90 minutes elapsed including pressure buildup. This is Sunday prep, with about 15 minutes active to reheat on a weeknight. Beans add roughly 10–14g protein per serving alongside the main.
